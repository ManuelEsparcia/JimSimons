Especificación: data/edgar/filings_flags.py

1. Propósito

Deriva flags y eventos a nivel de filing que son informativos para la
estrategia: late filings, restatements, NT filings, form type anomalías,
filing frequency anomalías, y red flags regulatorios. Estos flags
alimentan tanto point_in_time.py (quality scoring) como features/
(señales de riesgo informacional).

2. Flags canónicos (por filing)

Timeliness:
  is_late_filer: deadline_date < filed_date.
  days_to_deadline: filed_date - deadline_date (negativo = early).
  is_nt_filing: form_type in (NT 10-K, NT 10-Q).

Restatement / amendment:
  is_amended: form_type contiene "/A" (10-K/A, 10-Q/A).
  is_restatement: filing referencia un filing previo con misma
    fiscal period y diferentes valores materiales.

Form classification:
  form_family: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1, proxy, other.
  _classify_form_family(form_type, reverse_map) → family string.

Filing anomalies:
  filing_gap_days: días desde el filing anterior del mismo CIK.
  is_filing_gap_anomaly: gap > threshold (configurable).

3. Deadline inference

_infer_deadline_date(row, cfg) → (deadline, method):
  Large accelerated filer: 10-K 60d, 10-Q 40d after fiscal_period_end.
  Accelerated filer: 10-K 75d, 10-Q 40d.
  Non-accelerated: 10-K 90d, 10-Q 45d.
  Smaller reporting: 10-K 90d, 10-Q 45d.
  Default si filer_status desconocido: non-accelerated.

4. Inputs

submissions.parquet: de fetch_submissions.
companyfacts.parquet: de fetch_companyfacts (para detectar restatements).
config: thresholds, form_family_map, deadline_rules.

5. Linking facts to filings

_link_facts_to_filings(submissions, facts, failures):
  Join por accession_number. Facts sin filing → orphan flag.
  Filing sin facts → empty_filing flag.

6. Rolling statistics

_rolling_flag_mean(values, window): media móvil de flags binarios.
  Usado para detectar tendencias: CIK con late filings recurrentes.

_mad(values): Median Absolute Deviation para detección de outliers
  en filing_gap_days.

7. Output

flags.parquet: (cik, accession_number, form_type, form_family,
  filed_date, fiscal_period_end, deadline_date, days_to_deadline,
  is_late_filer, is_nt_filing, is_amended, filing_gap_days,
  is_filing_gap_anomaly, flag_summary_score).

8. Tests mínimos

- 10-K filed 100d after FPE for large accelerated → is_late_filer=True.
- 10-K/A → is_amended=True, form_family="10-K".
- NT 10-Q → is_nt_filing=True.
- Filing gap > threshold → is_filing_gap_anomaly=True.
- Deadline inference: non-accelerated 10-K → 90 days.
- Facts linked to filing por accession_number.
